Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kick AI Clipper handles information when creators use the application and connect supported social media accounts.

Information We Process

When a creator connects a TikTok account, the application may process information made available through the permissions granted by the creator, including basic TikTok account information and authorization tokens required to access approved TikTok features.

How Information Is Used

Information obtained through TikTok is used only to provide the requested integration features, such as identifying the authorized account and uploading or publishing creator-selected video content.

Authentication

TikTok authentication is performed using TikTok's official authorization system. Kick AI Clipper does not request or store the creator's TikTok password.

Video Content

Video files processed by the application are used to prepare and publish content requested by the creator.

Sharing of Information

Information obtained through TikTok is not sold to third parties. Information is transmitted to TikTok when necessary to perform creator-authorized TikTok features.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect authentication credentials and other information processed by the application.

User Control

Creators may revoke the application's TikTok access through their TikTok account settings.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features or integrations change.
