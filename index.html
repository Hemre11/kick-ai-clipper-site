Kick AI Clipper

A desktop tool for preparing and publishing short-form video clips.

About

Kick AI Clipper helps creators process long-form livestream recordings into short-form video clips.

The application can prepare video clips, generate subtitles, format videos for vertical viewing, organize publishing schedules, and publish content to supported social media platforms after the creator authorizes their account.

TikTok Integration

Kick AI Clipper uses TikTok Login Kit to allow a creator to securely authorize their TikTok account.

After authorization, the Content Posting API can be used to upload or publish video content selected by the creator to their authorized TikTok account.

The application does not request a creator's TikTok password. TikTok account authorization is performed through TikTok's official authorization flow.

Creator Control

Creators remain responsible for the content they choose to process and publish. TikTok access can be revoked through the creator's account settings.
